FAQ

Common questions

How long does one video take?

About 8 minutes from topic to saved file in the latest run. Longer videos take longer to render.

Can it make and upload a video every day?

Yes. On the Automation page choose Every day, set a time, and either list your topics (used in order) or let AI pick topics for your niche. You can also post on weekdays only or once a week, and set a stop date.

Where do I put my API keys?

On the Automation page, the first time you open it. Click Get my key, copy the key on the page that opens, then come back and allow the paste. They are saved in your browser, so you enter them only once.

Can I upload and schedule videos I already have?

Yes. On the Automation page open the Upload & Schedule Videos tab, choose one or many video files, and either let AI write the titles and descriptions or write your own. Then upload now, or schedule them with a gap between each video.

Can I connect more than one YouTube channel?

Yes. Click Add another account on the Automation page and choose a different Google account. Then pick which channel each video goes to.

How many videos can I upload each day?

That depends on your YouTube API quota. New Google Cloud projects usually allow only a few uploads a day, and you can request more in Google Cloud Console.

Why are my uploads private?

YouTube may keep uploads from unaudited API projects private. Check them in YouTube Studio, and change visibility there once your project is approved.

What if a video fails to render?

The run stops and logs the error. Other scheduled topics are not affected, and you can run the topic again from Make.
